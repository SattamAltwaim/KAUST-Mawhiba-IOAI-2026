# Rules

1. **Individual work.** No team merging. You may discuss general strategies with classmates but all code must be your own.

2. **Pretrained models required.** You must use at least one pretrained model (e.g. EfficientNet, ResNet, ConvNeXt, ViT). Training from scratch is not allowed.

3. **No test label leakage.** Do not look up the original dataset's folder structure to obtain test labels. Do not hard-code predictions.

4. **Submission limits.** Maximum **5 submissions per day.** Trust your cross-validation score, not the public leaderboard.

5. **Allowed libraries.** PyTorch, torchvision, timm, scikit-learn, albumentations, PIL/Pillow, OpenCV, numpy, pandas. If you want to use something else, ask first.

6. **Do not modify the submission generator function** in the baseline notebook. It ensures your IDs match the expected format.

7. **Final ranking** is based on the **private leaderboard**, revealed after the competition ends. The public leaderboard only scores a subset of the test data.
