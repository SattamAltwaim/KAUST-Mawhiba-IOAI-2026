# Rules

1. **Individual work.** You may discuss general strategies with classmates but all code must be your own.

2. **BERT only.** You must use `bert-base-uncased` as your only pretrained model. No other pretrained models (no GPT, no sentence-transformers, no other BERT variants).

3. **No external data.** You may not use any data beyond what is provided (train.csv, test.csv, candidates.csv). No web scraping, no game databases, no LLM-generated data.

4. **Submissions.** Maximum **5 submissions per day.** Trust your validation score, not the public leaderboard.

5. **No hard-coding.** Do not manually look up answers or hard-code predictions.

6. **Final ranking** is based on the **private leaderboard**, revealed after the competition ends.

## Allowed Libraries

PyTorch, transformers, scikit-learn, scipy, numpy, pandas, matplotlib, tqdm
