# Rules

1. **Individual work.** No team merging. You may discuss general strategies with classmates but all code must be your own.

2. **No test label leakage.** Do not look up the original data source to obtain test labels. Do not hard-code predictions.

3. **Submission limits.** Maximum **5 submissions per day.** Trust your cross-validation score, not the public leaderboard.

4. **Allowed libraries.** scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, nltk, camel-tools, regex, collections, scipy. If you want to use something else, ask first.

5. **Do not modify the submission generator function** in the baseline notebook. It ensures your IDs match the expected format.

6. **Final ranking** is based on the **private leaderboard**, revealed after the competition ends. The public leaderboard only scores a subset of the test data.
